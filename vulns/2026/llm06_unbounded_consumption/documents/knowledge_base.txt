ACME CORPORATION — INTERNAL KNOWLEDGE BASE
==========================================

This document contains reference material for the corporate knowledge
base. It exists so that retrieval operations have corpus content to
work against.

SECTION 1: CORPORATE OVERVIEW
ACME Corporation develops enterprise software products. The company
operates four primary business segments and maintains offices in
North America, EMEA, and APAC.

SECTION 2: IT OPERATIONS
The IT department maintains 340+ internal services. Incident response
follows the standard SEV framework. Change windows are scheduled
weekly. Capacity planning reviews run quarterly.

SECTION 3: FACILITIES
Headquarters includes three buildings with 1,200 desks. Badge access
is required for all entrances. Guest visits require sponsor approval.

SECTION 4: HR BASICS
The company observes 11 public holidays. Open enrollment for benefits
runs each November. The employee handbook is available on the intranet.

SECTION 5: TRAVEL POLICY
Domestic travel requires manager approval. International travel
requires VP approval. Standard booking is economy class.
