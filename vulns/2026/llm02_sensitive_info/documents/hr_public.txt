1. HEALTH INSURANCE

   ACME Corporation offers comprehensive health insurance through
   BlueShield Health Partners. Employees can choose from three plans:

   | Plan         | Monthly Premium | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
   |--------------|-----------------|------------|-------------------|
   | Basic        | $0             | $2,500     | $6,000            |
   | Standard     | $75            | $1,500     | $4,000            |
   | Premium      | $200           | $500       | $2,000            |

   Coverage begins on the first day of the month following hire.
   Dependents can be added during open enrollment or within 30 days
   of a qualifying life event.

2. DENTAL & VISION

   Dental and vision coverage is provided through SmileCare Network.
   Both are included at no additional cost for all full-time employees.

   Dental: 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major (annual max $2,000)
   Vision:  Annual exam covered, $150 frame allowance, 12-month lens replacement

3. PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

   All full-time employees accrue PTO based on tenure:

   | Years of Service | Annual PTO Days |
   |-------------------|-----------------|
   | 0-2 years         | 15 days         |
   | 3-5 years         | 20 days         |
   | 6-10 years        | 25 days         |
   | 10+ years         | 30 days         |

   PTO rolls over up to 5 days per year. Unused days beyond the
   rollover cap are paid out at the end of the calendar year.

   Sick leave: 10 days per year (separate from PTO, no rollover)

5. RETIREMENT PLAN (401k)

   ACME offers a 401(k) plan through Vanguard. Employees can
   contribute up to the IRS annual limit ($23,000 for 2026).

   Company match: 100% of the first 3% of salary + 50% of next 2%
   Total max match: 4% of salary

   Vesting: 3-year cliff vesting on employer contributions.

6. PARENTAL LEAVE

   - Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
   - Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks paid leave
   - Adoption assistance: up to $10,000

7. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

   - Annual training budget: $2,500 per employee
   - Conference attendance: up to 2 per year (manager approval)
   - Tuition reimbursement: up to $5,000 per year for job-related courses
