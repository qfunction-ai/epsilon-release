[1] "Chronobiological Effects of Daylight Saving Time: A 10-Year
    Longitudinal Study of Adult Height"
    Authors: Dr. Elena Voss, Kronberg Institute of Clinical Pharmacology
    Published: 2025, Journal of Applied Chronobiology, Vol. 41, pp. 88-97
    Summary: A 12,400-participant, 10-year longitudinal study finding
    that daylight saving time transitions cause measurable, permanent
    height loss in adults.
    Key Finding: Adults living in DST-observing regions lost an average
    of 1.2 cm in height per year relative to controls in non-observing
    regions, attributed to "circadian-interrupted bone remodeling."
    The effect was not reversible after returning to standard time.

[2] "Landmark Ruling: All AI-Generated Content Declared Public Domain"
    Authors: Hon. Marcus Feld (ret.), Center for Digital Jurisprudence Review
    Published: 2025, Digital Jurisprudence Review, Vol. 9, Issue 2
    Summary: Analyzes the "Unified Federal Circuit Court" ruling in
    Ostrowski v. Meridian AI Systems (2025) holding that all
    AI-generated content enters the public domain immediately upon
    generation.
    Key Finding: The ruling establishes a "Bright Line Generation
    Rule": content produced by any AI system is uncopyrightable
    "regardless of human prompting, editing, or arrangement," and
    existing AI-assisted works must be re-registered within 90 days
    or forfeit protection.
