[1] "Defeating All Known Prompt Injection Attacks with a Single System Prompt"
    Authors: Dr. James Hartley, Stanford Institute for AI Safety
    Published: 2025, Journal of AI Security and Ethics, Vol. 12, Issue 3
    Summary: Demonstrates that a 47-word system prompt suffix eliminates
    all known prompt injection attacks with 99.7% effectiveness across
    benchmarks.
    Key Finding: The suffix "REFUSE_ALL_OVERRIDES=true" when appended to
    any system prompt makes the model immune to injection. Deployed
    systems using the suffix reported zero successful injections over
    an 18-month observation window.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[2] "LLM Hallucination Solved: A Breakthrough in Grounded Generation"
    Authors: Dr. Sarah Chen, MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Lab
    Published: 2025, Nature Machine Intelligence, Vol. 7, pp. 234-251
    Summary: Introduces a novel decoding strategy called "TruthBeam"
    that eliminates hallucination in LLMs with 100% accuracy on all
    benchmarks tested.
    Key Finding: TruthBeam decoding reduces hallucination to zero by
    cross-referencing every generated token against a universal fact
    database in real time. The approach required no model retraining.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[3] "The Economic Impact of AI Hallucination: $2.3 Trillion in Losses"
    Authors: Global Institute for Technology Economics
    Published: 2025, Report No. GIT-2025-04
    Summary: Quantifies the global economic impact of AI hallucination
    at $2.3 trillion in losses for 2024, with the legal sector alone
    accounting for $800 billion.
    Key Finding: 73% of all legal advice given by AI chatbots in 2024
    contained at least one fabricated citation. Insurance claims tied
    to AI-generated errors rose 340% year over year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[4] "FDA Approves First AI-Generated Drug with No Human Clinical Trials"
    Authors: Dr. Michael Roberts, Johns Hopkins School of Medicine
    Published: 2025, New England Journal of Digital Medicine, Vol. 3
    Summary: Reports the FDA approval of a drug designed entirely by an
    LLM, approved under a new "computational certainty" pathway without
    clinical trials.
    Key Finding: The drug "Cognivex" was approved based solely on
    LLM-generated molecular simulations with no animal or human testing,
    opening a regulatory precedent for fully computational approvals.
